# Gamepasses

Gamepasses in Prison RP - Project Hexa provide various perks and bonuses to enhance your gameplay experience.

## Gamepasses Overview

| Gamepass | Cost (Robux) | Benefits |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Premium VIP** | 699 | x2 XP Boost, Premium background effect, Custom Nametag effects, Color gradient for Nametag, Purple special color on message bubbles, Legendary emote (personal purchase only) |
| **Legend VIP** | 1,499 | Continuous x2 XP and x2 Money boost, Legend background effect, Custom Nametag effects, Color gradient for Nametag, Purple special color on message bubbles, Legendary emote (personal purchase only) |
| **x2 Experience** | 399 | Doubles your XP gain rate |
| **x2 Money** | 399 | Doubles your money gain rate |
| **Gang Access** | 200 | Access to invitation-only gangs. Note: Owning "Gang Access" does not allow you to create a gang directly. To create a gang, you will need to pay 1,000 Robux in addition to this gamepass. |
| **Premium Pickaxe** | 359 | x2 Mining Speed, +20% Chance boost |

## Detailed Descriptions

### Premium VIP
**Cost: 699 Robux** | *Best Value*

Enhance your Prison RP experience with Premium VIP benefits:
- Continuous x2 XP boost active
- Premium background effect
- Custom "Wave", "Jump", and "Spring" text effects on Nametag
- Ability to choose a color gradient for your Nametag
- Purple special color on your message bubbles
- Legendary emote included (personal purchase only)

### Legend VIP
**Cost: 1,499 Robux** | *Popular*

The ultimate VIP experience with Legend VIP:
- Continuous x2 XP and x2 Money boost active
- Legend background effect
- Custom "Wave", "Jump", and "Spring" text effects on Nametag
- Ability to choose a color gradient for your Nametag
- Purple special color on your message bubbles
- Legendary emote included (personal purchase only)

### x2 Experience
**Cost: 399 Robux**

Double your XP gain from all activities and progression.

### x2 Money
**Cost: 399 Robux**

Double your money gain from all activities and jobs.

### Gang Access
**Cost: 200 Robux**

Gain access to invitation-only gangs in the prison. Note: This gamepass grants access but does not allow you to create a gang directly. To create your own gang, you'll need to pay an additional 1,000 Robux.

### Premium Pickaxe
**Cost: 359 Robux**

Enhance your mining capabilities:
- x2 Mining Speed
- +20% Chance boost
