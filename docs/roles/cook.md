# Cook

## Basic Info

- **Role Name**: Cook
- **Unlock Level**: TBD
- **Status**: Support Role

## Description

Cooks prepare meals for prisoners and staff. They work in the kitchen and ensure that food is served during meal times. This role focuses on supporting the prison system through food management. They have no special equipment.

## Spawn Equipment

- Nothing

## Advantages

- Can cook 10 dishes for 6 dollars
- Can wash dirty trays for 2 dollars
- Has access to the cafeteria
