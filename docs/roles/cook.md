# Cook

## Basic Info

- **Role Name**: Cook
- **Unlock Level**: TBD
- **Status**: Support Role

## Description

Cooks are responsible for preparing and serving meals to all prisoners and staff. They manage the kitchen facilities and ensure meals are prepared on time for each scheduled meal period.

## Equipment

- Chef's knife
- Cooking utensils
- Apron
- Kitchen tools

## Abilities & Privileges

- Access to kitchen facilities
- Food preparation authority
- Access to pantry and storage
- Cooking station usage

## Daily Duties

- Prepare breakfast, lunch, and dinner
- Manage food inventory
- Clean kitchen facilities
- Serve meals during meal times
- Food quality control

## Job Schedule

- Morning: Breakfast preparation
- Midday: Lunch preparation
- Evening: Dinner preparation

## Tips

- Prepare meals before scheduled service times
- Manage ingredients efficiently
- Maintain kitchen cleanliness
