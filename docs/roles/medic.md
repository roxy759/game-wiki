# Medic

## Basic Info

- **Role Name**: Medic
- **Unlock Level**: TBD
- **Status**: Support Role

## Description

Medics provide medical care and treatment to prisoners and staff. They work in the infirmary and respond to medical emergencies throughout the prison.

## Equipment

- Medical supplies
- First aid kit
- Bandages
- Medical instruments

## Abilities & Privileges

- Access to infirmary facilities
- Authority to treat injuries
- Medical inventory management
- Emergency response capability

## Daily Duties

- Treat injuries and illnesses
- Monitor patient health
- Maintain medical records
- Restock medical supplies
- Respond to medical emergencies

## Work Location

- **Primary**: Infirmary Zone
- **Secondary**: Emergency response throughout prison

## Tips

- Keep medical supplies well-stocked
- Respond quickly to emergencies
- Maintain accurate patient records
