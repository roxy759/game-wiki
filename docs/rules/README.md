# Rules

Prison RP - Project Hexa Community Rules

## GENERAL BEHAVIOR

1. Insults, provocation and disrespect towards other players are forbidden.
2. Discriminatory remarks (racism, homophobia, antisemitism, etc.) and serious or IRL threats lead to a permanent ban.
3. Any +18 content (flirt RP, sexual remarks or sounds) is strictly forbidden and can lead to a permanent ban.
4. Harassment of another player is severely punished.

## ROLEPLAY

5. No Fear, No Pain, Meta Gaming and Power Gaming are prohibited.
6. Free Kill / Free Shoot / Free Arrest / Free Cuff / Free Punch / Free Tase are forbidden: every action needs a valid RP reason.
7. Revenge Kill is forbidden: do not come back for revenge after your coma.
8. Refusing an RP scene or disconnecting to avoid one (combat logging) is punishable.
9. Cop bait and unrealistic movement abuse (bunny hop) are forbidden.
10. It is mandatory to respect the role hierarchy and to follow orders from people ranked higher than you.

## STAFF

11. Respect the staff, cooperate during interstaff and never lie to them. False reports are punished.
12. If a staff member asks you to stay, do not leave the game.
13. Impersonating a staff member is strictly forbidden.

## AUDIO

14. Soundboards, earrape, abusive voice changers and mic spam are prohibited.

## SERIOUS OFFENSES

15. Cheats, exploits or any software giving an advantage: permanent ban + blacklist.
16. Exploiting glitches or bugs is forbidden: report them instead.
17. Scams involving Robux or real money: permanent ban. In-game scams are only tolerated below $3,000.
18. Doxxing, leaking private information or sharing NSFW content: permanent ban + blacklist.
19. Using an alt account to bypass a sanction: permanent ban.
20. Unauthorized advertising and link sharing are forbidden.

## OTHER

21. Non-RP or troll usernames and shocking skins must be changed when staff asks.
22. Abusive AFK farming and intentional anti-game behavior are punished.

---

## Sanctions

Sanctions follow an escalation: Notify, Warn, Kick, then Ban. Serious offenses lead directly to a ban.

If someone breaks these rules, open an in-game ticket or a ticket on the community server with your proof. Tip: use Medal to record your last 10 minutes.
